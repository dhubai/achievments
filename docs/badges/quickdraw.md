# Quickdraw

Close an issue or pull request **within ~5 minutes** of opening it (same repo).

## Practice setup (your repo)

1. Create a private or public test repo under your account.
2. Open a trivial issue or PR.
3. Close it within 5 minutes (merge or close).

Only the **first** tier is commonly documented; higher tiers are unclear.

## Notes

- Timing is strict; use a clock.
- Works on your own sandbox repo; no need to bother other maintainers.
